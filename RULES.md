# Rules as implemented

Cripple Mr Onion first appears in Terry Pratchett's *Witches Abroad*. Playable rules were posted to USENET around 1993 by Andrew Millard and Terence Tao; the full text is kept on the L-Space wiki: <https://wiki.lspace.org/Cripple_Mr_Onion_Full_Rules>.

This version follows those rules where the sources agree. Where they disagree or leave a gap, the choice made is listed under **Interpretations** so it can be checked against the full text and corrected.

## The deck

104 cards: a French-suited pack and a Spanish-suited pack shuffled together.

| Suit-pair | French | Spanish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Spades | Swords |
| 2 | Clubs | Staves |
| 3 | Hearts | Cups |
| 4 | Diamonds | Coins |

Card values are as in Blackjack: Ace = 1 or 11, J/Q/K = 10, others face value.

## A hand

1. Everyone puts the **Stake** (2 chips) into the pot.
2. **First deal:** five cards each, face down.
3. **Discard:** from the Elder (left of the Dealer) round to the Dealer, each player may swap up to four cards.
4. **First betting round.**
5. **Second deal:** five more cards each, face up, except the Dealer's, which are face down.
6. **Second betting round.**
7. **Showdown:** from the Elder, each player still in must display one grouping from their ten cards that beats the grouping on the table, or throw in. The last grouping standing wins the pot.

If nobody can show anything, the pot carries over to the next hand. The deal passes to the left each hand. The game ends when you, or everyone else, runs out of chips.

## Groupings, lowest to highest

| Grouping | Made of |
| --- | --- |
| Bagel | Two cards totalling 20 |
| Two-card Onion | Two cards totalling 21 (Ace + picture) |
| Broken Flush | 3+ cards totalling 16–21, all but one in the same suit-pair |
| Three-card Onion | Three cards totalling 21 |
| Flush | 3+ cards totalling 16–21, all in the same suit-pair |
| Four-card Onion | Four cards totalling 21 |
| Broken Royal | 6, 7, 8 of any suits |
| Five-card Onion | Five cards totalling 21 |
| Royal | 7, 7, 7 |
| Six-card Onion | Six cards totalling 21 |
| Wild Royal | 8, 8, 8 when eights are wild |
| Seven-card Onion | Seven cards totalling 21 (there is no eight-card Onion) |
| Double Onion | Two Aces and two picture cards |
| Triple Onion | Three Aces and three picture cards |
| Lesser Onion | Four Aces and four picture cards |
| Great Onion | Five Aces and five picture cards |

## Crippling

A **running flush** is a run of consecutive ranks all in one suit-pair (Ace may be low or high). It is worthless on its own, but:

- a **nine-card** running flush played on a **Great Onion** cripples it;
- a **ten-card** running flush cripples a Great Onion **or** a Lesser Onion.

Crippling ends the hand immediately and the crippler takes the pot.

## Optional modifier: Null Eights (on by default)

An eight may count as 0 instead of 8. If anyone shows a grouping that does this, **eights are wild in the next hand** (and Null Eights can't be used during it). A wild eight can stand for any card.

Between two Onions of the same size, the one using fewer null eights wins. With eights wild, a group of eight eights counts as a Lesser Onion that beats other Lesser Onions, but can be crippled like a Great Onion.

## Interpretations

These are the calls this version makes where the sources were unclear. They were pieced together from secondary sources, because the full-rules page couldn't be fetched while this was being built, so they're the first thing to check against the original text.

- **Flushes and running flushes use suit-pairs**, not exact suits.
- **A grouping must strictly beat the one on the table.** An equal grouping doesn't count.
- **Flush tie-break:** the higher total wins.
- **The showdown is a single pass** from the Elder to the Dealer; nobody gets a second turn.
- **Betting** is fixed-limit: each raise is one Stake, with at most three raises per round. A player who can't cover a bet goes all in and can still win the whole pot (no side pots).
- **Any** null eight shown at the showdown, by anyone, makes eights wild next hand.
- **Not included yet:** the Lady modifier and the Caroc (Discworld tarot) deck variant.
